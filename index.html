GEOPOLITICS Strait of Hormuz Blockade In Effect — US-Iran Talks Collapse, Military Escalation Risk Rises
Trump announced a naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz after weekend peace talks in Islamabad broke down without a deal. Iran is demanding control of the Strait, war reparations, and release of frozen assets. Mediators from Pakistan, Egypt, and Turkey will continue talks over the coming days. Trump is weighing limited military strikes, per WSJ. The blockade went into effect Monday — U.S. Central Command said it won't block vessels bound for non-Iranian ports. This is the critical variable for oil markets.
